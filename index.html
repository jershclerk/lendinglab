Eligibility

Who can apply

A short list of requirements to get you started. Final eligibility is determined by the lender of record at application.

For small businesses

  • A U.S.-based business, incorporated or operating as a sole proprietor
  • An active business bank account
  • At least a modest revenue history (most terms accept early-stage operations)
  • A legal representative who is 18 or older

For 501(c)(3) charities & nonprofits

  • A registered 501(c)(3) or other recognized nonprofit, charitable or religious organization in good standing
  • An organization bank account under your legal name
  • Current tax filings (most recent 990 or equivalent)
  • A legal representative who is 18 or older

Requirements are illustrative; some programs accept applicants with shorter operating histories. Final eligibility — and the rate, term, and total disclosed to you — is determined by the lender of record at application.

FAQ

Questions you’re probably asking

Does checking my options cost anything? +
No. Checking your options on Lending Lab is free, and does not create or check your business credit. A lender of record may run a credit inquiry only if you apply and consent.
Can 501(c)(3) charities apply? +
Yes. We serve registered charities and 501(c)(3) organizations as well as small businesses. Eligible uses include program funding, operating liquidity, payroll, and reserves — subject to underwriting.
Who actually extends the loan? +
Lending Lab is powered by Chelcie, a capital-coordination layer. Our lending partners set credit terms, approve or decline applications, disburse funds, and receive repayment. Chelcie matches you to available capital — including loans, grants, and eligible programs.
How fast can I get funds? +
Most applications are reviewed within a day, and first funds often arrive within 1–2 business days after approval. These are indicative targets only; actual timing depends on underwriting, verification, and disbursement.
What can I use the loan for? +
Generally: working capital, inventory, equipment, seasonal bridging, and — for nonprofits — program and operating liquidity. Permitted and prohibited uses are disclosed precisely in your quote. Some loan programs may restrict specific uses.
What if I’m not approved? +
A declination does not obligate you to anything. You’ll receive a clear reason when available, and you’re welcome to apply again or revisit when your circumstances change. There is no fee for a declination.
Is this an offer of credit? +
No. Amounts, rates, terms, and timing shown on this page are for illustration only and do not constitute an offer, commitment, or pre-qualification of credit. The binding terms — including APR, fees, and total of payments — are disclosed in the application before you agree to anything.
Will you sell my data? +
We do not sell your data. Information you provide is used to coordinate your capital access, process your application through our lending partners, and keep you safe — in line with our Privacy Policy. See privacy@chelcie.app for questions.
